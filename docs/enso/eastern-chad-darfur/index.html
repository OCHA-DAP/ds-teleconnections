Season and Darfur cereal yields

Sorghum and millet, traditional rainfed farming, summed over the Darfur states. Yield as % of the median of the surrounding years (five either side, same source: Ministry of Agriculture to 2015, FAO crop assessment from 2016), because the level of the series shifts several times. Grey crosses: 2018 and 2019, which report the same harvested area to the hectare, left out of the correlation. 2014 and 2015 look high but have no documented problem, so they stay in; a rank correlation keeps them from driving the result. No such series exists for eastern Chad.

How the indicators and outcomes move together, seasons before 2023

Rank correlation (Spearman) between seasons; the small figure is the number of seasons in common. Outcomes of the lean season are matched to the season before it, and use seasons up to 2021 (lean season 2022); the rest use seasons up to 2022. Grey: fewer than 8 seasons. With 10–12 seasons, only values beyond about ±0.6 stand out from chance. Vegetation and rain are detrended as above; FAO ASI is a share of cropland under stress, so its signs run opposite to the other indicators. Darfur yields leave out 2018–2019.
